# The Abyss

A new cave layer below the deepslate. The world now goes down to **bedrock at y=−128**, and everything between y=−64
and the bedrock is the Abyss: big open caverns cut through **abyssal stone**.

## Getting there

Dig down. Below the deepslate (around y=−64) the stone turns slate-blue. Like deepslate, the Abyss has its own band of
the world (four chunk sections, y=−128 to −65), with a short blend where it meets the deepslate.

**In an existing world**, only chunks generated after you add the mod get the Abyss. Explore somewhere new to find it.
The layer comes from a built-in data pack, **Deep Caves**, which is on by default. You can switch it off in a new
world's *Data Packs* screen to keep the vanilla depth.

Your ores don't move: everything vanilla places (diamonds, redstone, ancient cities and the rest) stays at the same
heights and densities as before. The Abyss adds ores of its own on top.

## The five caverns

Which cavern you're in follows the climate of the land above, and a belt of ordinary rocky cavern always lies
between one kind and the next.

| Cavern | Found under | What's there | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abyssal Caverns** | Most places, and between the others | Huge rocky chambers, dripstone, tuff, gravel and glow lichen | The usual cave mobs |
| **Toxic Caves** | Dry lands | Pools of **toxic sludge** across about a fifth of the floor, glowing **toxic moss** creeping over ceilings and floors, tuff and gravel | Sludge slows and poisons you; lots of slimes |
| **Frozen Caverns** | Cold lands | Like the ice spikes biome underground: great **ice spires** rising from the floor and hanging from the ceiling (now and then a giant), over patches of packed ice, snow and bare rock | Strays instead of skeletons; powder snow traps |
| **Sunken Wilds** | Wet lands | An underground overworld: moss, grass, azaleas, cave vines and spore blossoms under a bright **lumen moss** ceiling | Axolotls and glow squid live here too |
| **Hollow Dark** | Mountains and rugged hills | Sculk-choked caverns, like the deep dark | Sculk sensors and shriekers. Tread quietly |

Water springs and lava springs run through all of them, and the bottom few levels (below y=−118) flood with **lava**,
like the floor of the vanilla caves.

## Blocks

| Block | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Abyssal stone** | Mine it (needs a pickaxe). Tougher than deepslate |
| **Cobbled abyssal stone** | What abyssal stone drops. Smelt it back into abyssal stone |
| **Polished abyssal stone** | 4 abyssal stone, or the stonecutter |
| **Abyssal bricks** | 4 polished abyssal stone, or the stonecutter |
| **Toxic sludge** | The Toxic Caves' floor. Glows faintly, and slows and poisons anything standing in it |
| **Toxic moss** | Glows green on the Toxic Caves' ceilings |
| **Lumen moss** | The Sunken Wilds' ceiling. Nearly as bright as glowstone, enough to grow plants by |

## Ores

The Abyss gets its own iron, gold, coal, redstone and lapis veins, tuff pockets, and extra **diamond veins** anywhere
between y=−124 and −70. Bring an iron pickaxe or better.
